Privacy Policy — Rombik Games

Effective date: 14 October 2026

In short:
  • Rombik Games does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data — neither the child's nor the parent's.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party data-collecting SDKs.
  • The app requests no device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • The app itself does not connect to the internet. The one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile app Rombik Games (App Store and Google Play, bundle ID com.rombik.toddlersgame), published by Daria Kolpakova (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to daka.kolp01@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no registration, no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content that leaves the device, and no forms for entering a name, e-mail, age or any other personal detail.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your choices between launches, the app keeps a few settings in its private storage on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

SettingWhy
Light or dark look of the parents' pageTo keep the chosen appearance
Music and sounds on or offTo keep the chosen sound settings
Mode of the colouring gameTo reopen the game the way it was left
Whether the full version has been purchasedTo keep the paid games open, even offline

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

4. In-app purchase

Some games are opened by a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase (and its restoration) is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive or see your name, payment card, e-mail or other billing details; the app only learns from the store whether the purchase is owned.

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases can only be started from the parents' area, which is opened by holding a button and is designed to be used by an adult.

5. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics or crash-reporting services, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not share or sell any data to third parties. It contains no links to websites or social networks that a child could open.

6. Children's privacy

Rombik Games is made for young children. Because we collect no personal information at all, we comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including Article 8), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, as well as the Apple Kids Category and Google Play Families requirements. No parental consent is needed, since no data is collected.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. As we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access or delete on our side; all on-device settings disappear when the app is removed. You may still contact us with any question.

8. Security

Settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and no data is transmitted over the network by the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to process data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before such a version is released.

10. Contact
